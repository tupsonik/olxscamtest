# 2026 Research Notes

## Market
The 2026 Polish e-commerce report from Izba Gospodarki Elektronicznej says 93% of internet users bought online in the previous six months and 97% of purchases involve an internet-connected device at some stage, particularly smartphones. This supports a mobile-first workflow.

## Threat landscape
UOKiK highlights fake stores, impersonation of Polish brands and hidden dropshipping. OLX advises users to stay in the platform chat, avoid external payment links and use platform delivery/payment or cash on delivery where possible. Vinted states that its buyer protection applies to purchases made through "Kup teraz" and that purchases outside that flow do not receive the same protection.

Google is expanding AI-based scam protection in Android in 2026, including protections against impersonation and suspicious application behavior. This validates the problem space, but also means Nie Wtop must solve a narrower problem than generic Android scam detection: helping a buyer evaluate an offer immediately before payment.

## Competitive gap to pursue
Generic website checkers already provide trust scores. Nie Wtop should differentiate with:
1. Polish marketplace context.
2. Evidence-first explanations.
3. Screenshot/share workflow.
4. Offer-specific checks, not only domain reputation.
5. A clear distinction between "unknown", "warning signs" and "confirmed external evidence".
